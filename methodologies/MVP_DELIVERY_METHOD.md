# MVP Delivery Method --- Sanitized Portable Specification

## Principle

> The MVP is finished when agreed evidence demonstrates the Definition
> of Done---not when there are no remaining improvements.

MVP completion does not imply production readiness.

## Lifecycle

1.  **Frame:** objective, value, constraints, authority,
    scope/out-of-scope, assumptions, DoD.
2.  **Research:** explicit questions, evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs,
    unknowns.
3.  **Architecture:** simplest design capable of proving DoD.
4.  **Plan:** testable increments with acceptance evidence.
5.  **Build:** controlled implementation preserving working baselines.
6.  **Test:** required behavior, assumptions, improvements and
    regressions.
7.  **Evaluate:** map evidence to every DoD criterion.
8.  **Freeze:** establish MVP baseline and stop unapproved expansion.
9.  **Reproduce/Handoff:** preserve setup, evidence, limitations and
    recovery.
10. **Closeout:** outcomes, lessons, recommendations, limitations,
    artifact index and handoff.

## DoD results

Pass; Pass with known limitation; Fail; Not tested; Out of scope.

## Change rule

A plausible change is not an improvement until evidence supports
retaining it.

## Anti-rabbit-hole check

Which DoD criterion does this support? What decision will it enable?
What evidence determines keep/reject? What is the stopping condition?
What baseline could it destabilize? If unclear, park it.

## AI behavior

Never invent requirements, claim unperformed tests or expand scope
merely because improvement is possible. Help the owner understand and
defend decisions.

ExecutionOS manages state/governance; this method manages delivery.
